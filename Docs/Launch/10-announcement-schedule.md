# Suggested announcement schedule
Relative days; select dates after the launch checklist passes. Times should use America/Chicago. This is a proposal, not a scheduled automation.

- Day -7 to -3: test browser workflows, resolve blockers, prepare screenshots and review disclosures.
- Day -2: invite a small group to test concrete workflows and fix reported failures.
- Day 0: choose one primary launch channel (Product Hunt or Show HN); remain available to respond.
- Day +1 or +2: share a candid r/SideProject introduction if current rules permit.
- Day +3: submit accurate AlternativeTo and SaaSHub information.
- Day +5: share engineering lessons with Indie Hackers and DEV Community.
- Day +7: review feedback, publish fixes and update application limitations.
- Later: consider r/opensource only after licensing is resolved and community requirements are met.

Space announcements to leave time for support. Do not imply that this schedule is a platform-recommended optimal posting time.
